Episode 127: Trick or Treat! It's Halloween and ImaNewbie has decorated his front porch in preparation for the hoards of Trick or Treaters that will soon be beating a path to his door demanding sweets and other goodies.

Soon a steady stream of witches, goblins,
pirates,and all manner of cleverly-costumed
trick or treaters are banging on his door.

So take this as a warning. If Halloween night you hear a knock at the door and you open it to find a band of orcs outside, slam the door and call 911!
